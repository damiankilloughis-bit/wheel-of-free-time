# Handstand: Lesson 1 (done Oct 11, 2026)

**Drill:** Paul Twyman's fingertip drill, from [LEARN HANDSTAND BALANCE Use the Fingers!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oi0u_wumu90) (Paul Twyman, 3:18)

- Kick up with your back to the wall.
- Press your fingertips into the floor to peel your heels off the wall.
- Count down while your heels are off. Return to the wall, then repeat.

**Result:** some progress. **Main struggle:** underbalance. The feet/body fall back toward the kick side instead of staying over the hands. Lesson 2 works on that.
