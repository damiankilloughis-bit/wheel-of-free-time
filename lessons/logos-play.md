## Your 15 minutes

**0:00–3:00 Pick the plan**
- **Plan A (the main goal):** be first in line for the next Taylors show. Their FAQ says there are no rush tickets ("we believe in rewarding the Early Bird").
- **Plan B (a Logos date you can do now):** a backstage tour in Taylors. These run Tue/Thu at 10:00am, 2:30pm, or 3:30pm, are 60 minutes, and take up to 15 people. Choose from *World of Theatre* ($10 each), *Puppetry Arts* at their warehouse ($15 each; you see the puppets in action), or *History & Alumni* ($10 each).
- **Plan C (cozy night in):** rent a past Logos show on [Logos Theatre On Demand](https://watch.thelogostheatre.com/). The page lists "$7.99 Watch Now" and "$1.99 Rent"; check what each includes before paying.

**3:00–8:00 Check with Nina**
Text her something like: *"Want to go see a show at the Logos together when their next one comes out? And/or do the puppet backstage tour some Tue/Thu afternoon?"* Ask which works better for her: Friday 7pm or Saturday 7pm.

**8:00–13:00 Get the tickets / get on the list**
- **Call [(864) 268-9342](tel:+18642689342)** (box office, Mon–Fri 9:30–5:30) and ask: "When will the 2026–27 season and the next show be announced? Is there a Christmas show in Taylors this year?" If you're doing Plan B, book the tour on the same call. To book a tour you can also email information@thelogostheatre.com with your names, preferred date/time, group size (2), and which tour.
- Join the email list (the "Join Our Email List" link at the bottom of [thelogostheatre.com](https://thelogostheatre.com/)) so you hear the moment tickets open.
- When a show is announced, buy online from that show's page using its **"TICKETS"** button (it goes to their AudienceView/OvationTix box office). Pick the Fri or Sat 7pm show.

**13:00–15:00 Calendar + dinner**
- Put the date on both your calendars. Doors open **30 minutes before** the show, and Narnia-size shows run about **3 hours** (the last one was ~180 min). For a 7pm show, plan to arrive around 6:30 and be done around 10.
- Plan dinner beforehand, not after, since you'll get out late. Pick a spot near Taylors or Greer and make it a 5pm reservation.

## Know before you go (from the [FAQ](https://thelogostheatre.com/frequently-asked-questions/))
- **Parking:** free, in the lot around the theatre. Enter from School St or Stevenson St.
- **Late arrivals** wait for a break before they're seated. Until then you can watch on hall monitors.
- **No refunds or exchanges**, except for weather cancellations. Pick a date you're sure about.
- **Dress code:** none published. The only rule is "no shirt, no shoes, no entry".
- **Not allowed:** alcohol, bags over 16"×16"×8", and photos or recording during the show. You *can* take photos with the characters afterward.
- **Extras:** concessions cost $2–$5 (popcorn, pretzels, candy, coffee, and hot chocolate in winter). You can also add a **backstage pass** for $5–$15 to meet the cast and puppets after the show. Add it when you buy tickets or by phone.
- The theatre is Christian. It describes its mission as "making the Bible come alive in minds and hearts."
